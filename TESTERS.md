# AuraGuide tester note (0.7.0-beta)

Same APK for phone-only testers and friends with Meta glasses.

AuraGuide is **its own AI**. It does **not** take over or socket into Meta’s onboard assistant (“Hey Meta”). That AI stays on the glasses. This app uses the phone camera (and later, if Meta opens it, the glasses camera) and talks through speaker / earbuds / glasses audio.

## You (phone only)

1. Uninstall any older AuraGuide, then install `AuraGuide-beta.apk`.
2. Allow camera + mic. Turn volume up.
3. Tap **▶**. It watches quietly — it will not narrate the room.
4. Tap **Talk to Aura** or 👁 when you want a name or a read.
5. Settings → Voice — pick Aria / Juniper / Nova / Rowan / Sage.
6. Settings → Gets to know you — your name and anything it should remember.
7. Optional: pair Bluetooth earbuds in Android, then Settings → Choose camera, earbuds, or glasses.

## Friends with Meta glasses

1. Same APK. Pair glasses in Android Bluetooth (Meta app can stay installed).
2. AuraGuide → Choose camera, earbuds, or glasses → Meta glasses.
3. Start a session. **Voice** can play through the glasses like earbuds.
4. **Sight** is still the **phone camera**. Meta does not give third-party apps the glasses camera or the onboard Meta AI.

Ask testers: phone model, glasses or “phone only”, whether voice sounded human, whether it stayed quiet until asked, and one sentence on usefulness.
